## Project conventions (Phyto Elements)

- Read `README.md` first: stack, structure, themes, scroll video, forms, chatbot.
- All site copy lives in `src/content/*` and is read only through `src/lib/content.ts` (async, CMS-ready). Never hard-code product data in components.
- Styling: use theme tokens only (`bg-paper`, `text-heading`, `bg-accent`, `text-accent-text`, `.band`, `.card` …). No raw hex colours in components; colour routes live in `src/styles/themes.css`.
- Colour: when changing a brand token in `themes.css`, also update its Display P3 line at the end of the file. Depth/typography effects live in `src/styles/polish.css`.
- Buttons: `.btn` (primary, accent fill) and `.btn btn-ghost` (outline in `--accent-text`). Keep this consistent in every theme.
- Positioning: Phyto Elements sources extracts from qualified producers, tests every lot, and sells under its label only lots that pass. Quality is shown through rigorous, very high testing standards. The page and nav label stay "Manufacturing & Quality" (/manufacturing), but manufacturing is not the headline and the Garcinia plant is not a focus. Use "lot", not "batch".
- Never state how many products/extracts there are (no "six extracts", "five more", "all six"): the client keeps adding products. Use range wording ("every extract", "our range") and render lists from `products.ts`.
- Product codes (PE-01 …) stay in `products.ts` as internal ids only; the client does not want them shown anywhere on the site (cards, lightbox, hero, tables, structured data).
- Content governance: never add unverified specs, certifications, capacities, customer names or health claims. Product `spec` values are the client's representative specifications (Nov 2026; Bakuchiol from published supplier data); unset fields show "Shared on request". No packing, lead-time or batch/lot-number fields on product pages.
- Contact: the only public email is info@phytoelements.in; the address is "Hyderabad, India"; the company name is "Phyto Elements". No review notes, placeholders or pre-production text on pages (ReviewNote is no longer used).
- Quality wording: say lots are tested to "recognised global quality standards" (and beyond) — never "our own standards/specification".
- Motion preference: the site does NOT follow the OS `prefers-reduced-motion` (client wants motion on every device, incl. Macs with Reduce motion on). Use `calmMotion()` / `html.pe-calm` from `lib/motion-pref.ts`; the footer "Reduce motion" toggle was removed (client, Nov 19), so motion is always on; a stored calm preference is cleared at boot.
- No illustrations: use product photos (preloader product row, lightbox, cards). No LabArt / BotanicalArt / 3D molecule in pages. Exception the client wants kept: the leaf-to-Phyto-bottle art (`LeafDropArt`) in the preloader and the page transition.
- Motion: client components only; respect the site motion preference (`calmMotion()`); content must be visible at rest (no opacity: 0 waiting on scroll). Clean up GSAP with `useGSAP` / `ctx.revert()`. Effects must clean up (client navigation).
- Motion components: preloader (`motion/Preloader`, CSS only, every full load), hero (`hero/SpecimenHero`, canvas), `sections/QualityFunnel` (GSAP scrub), line art (`botanicals/*`, CSS scroll-driven), wave edges (`.wave-top`). Styles live in `src/styles/motion.css`.
- Live colour route: R10 "Verdan light" (`verdan`), set as `defaultTheme` in `src/config/site.ts` (R07 lablight was the previous default). All other routes stay defined (R01 to R09) and must not be removed; R05 Blue + green is the original client palette, do not change it. There is no on-page route switcher.
- Lab routes (labgreen, lablight, labday) share `src/styles/lab.css`; per-route scene colours are `--lab-*` / `--specimen-*` tokens in themes.css. They and the review compare page live in `src/styles/lab.css`, `hero/HeroScene.tsx` and `app/compare/`. Compare is review-only (404 unless NEXT_PUBLIC_SHOW_THEME_SWITCHER=true).
- Full-screen section mode: `motion/PanelScroll` makes every top-level `<section>` in `<main>` (or in a single wrapper div) a sticky full-screen panel, snaps one screen per wheel gesture (Lenis snap "lock"); each incoming section is held in place and uncovered by a scroll-driven circle from the screen centre with a ring on its edge, on every page (`.rip-ring`, `--rip-p`). Page-to-page navigation plays the leaf-to-bottle extract transition (`motion/RouteTransition`); full loads and refreshes play the preloader every time. New page sections must be `<section>` elements with an opaque background and a `.wrap` child. Off for reduced motion and in /compare panes.
- Whole site (since Nov 19): R10 `verdan` "Verdan light" is `defaultTheme` — every page and the footer are light; only /home-classic uses lablight (ROUTE_THEMES). Homepage ("/"): uses R10 `verdan` "Verdan light" (white + pale sage, white frosted glass, chartreuse, full-strength product photo hero with a bold white dial outlined in dark forest; from the client's Verdan Farm OS reference).  Page-specific routes are listed in `ROUTE_THEMES` (config/site.ts), applied before paint by `routeThemeBootScript` and on client navigation by `motion/RouteTheme`. Styles: `src/styles/verdan.css` on top of lab.css. Its hero lot animation runs in the slower "staged" mode (`--specimen-mode: staged`, `--specimen-outline` in the verdan tokens; timings in `SpecimenHero` `STAGED`): Lots in → Test (passing lots flash blue) → Passed, with the matching step label lit. The previous homepage design is kept hidden at `/home-classic` (lablight, noindex, not in the menu); `/althome` redirects to `/`. Section order on "/": hero, products (ProductCarousel), who we are, from source to seal (DockCarousel), how we work, our guarantee (quality checks), enquiry, contact. `/home-classic` keeps the old order.
- Logo: approved client artwork only, in `public/brand` (mark, wordmark, stacked logo, each with a `-reverse` version for dark surfaces) and `src/app/icon.png`, `apple-icon.png`, `favicon.ico`. `layout/Logo` shows the reversed set via the `.logo-neg` rules (footer, dark themes, lablight header over the hero). Do not reintroduce a traced or redrawn logo.
- Product pages (/products and /products/[slug]) carry no illustrative designs: no botanical line drawings, no illustrative 3D molecule, no LabArt icons or backdrops. Use the product photos.
- Lab line art: `botanicals/LabArt` draws testing, QC, document and logistics motifs (chromatogram, vials, COA, drums, shipping …). Use it as a step icon (`art` field on testPillars/operatingFunctions) or a faint backdrop (`<LabArt kind backdrop pos />` inside a `labart-host` section). Pick the motif that matches the section's copy.
- Wheel scrolling: `PanelScroll` steps one screen per wheel gesture (trackpad momentum is swallowed until the glide ends and the wheel goes quiet); touch uses Lenis snap. Tune `GLIDE` (1.1s) / `QUIET` / `STEP` there. Source to seal is one screen: homepage uses `SealPipeline` (whole journey at a glance, highlight walks across the steps), Manufacturing & Quality uses `SealStepper` (auto-playing steps, click/pause); both in `sections/SealViews.tsx` via `SourceToSeal layout`. The old pinned scroll-through (n+1 screens) remains only on /home-classic (`layout="scroll"`).
- Products page: product overview table first, then the carousel (drag with the mouse / swipe; thumbnails bottom right; `lib/useDragScroll`), then the finder. Sideways trackpad swipes are left to carousels by PanelScroll.
- Homepage products (since Nov 19): the same `products/ProductCarousel` as /products, in `section#products.products-stage` (client's choice). `sections/ProductShowcase` is kept but not used. Previously: `sections/ProductShowcase` (cards shown straight away — no intro animation or scroll hold; glass cards, lightbox with prev/next and 3D per product). Glass styling on the green band; neumorphic cards elsewhere.
- 3D: keep three.js behind `LazyScene` (dynamic import, viewport + reduced-motion gate).
- Deployment target is Cloudflare Workers via OpenNext. Do NOT enable `cacheComponents` / `use cache` (not reliable on the Workers runtime). Keep pages statically generatable; read env vars via `getEnv()` at request time.
- Before finishing a change: `npm run lint && npm run typecheck && npm run build`.
- Phones and tablets (below 1121px): app-style bottom tab bar `layout/MobileDock` (Home, Products, Quality, Contact, More sheet with every page, the extracts as photo tiles, Sample and email); the header keeps the logo and a "Request Sample" button. Phones, tablets and touch screens scroll natively: PanelScroll (sticky panels, circle reveal, wheel stepping) is desktop-only (`NATIVE_SCROLL` query); the reveal flashed on touch. Card grids marked `.rail` become horizontal swipe rails on phones (≤767px) with dots from `ui/RailDots`; add `rail` to any new card grid. Styles in `src/styles/mobile.css`. Never leave a card as image-only on small screens: keep the name and key line.
- Menu type: the header nav and dock use the site font (Hanken Grotesk, `--font-sans`, heavier weights), not mono.
- Product pages end with the full portfolio (`.pf-rail`, current extract marked "Viewing") plus Previous / Next buttons. The products overview is a flexible grid list (`.po-list`), not a fixed table.
- Looks: "Light / Gradient / Logo" switch in the top menu (desktop) and in the phone More sheet — never in the footer; (`ui/SkinToggle`, `lib/skin-pref`, stored on the device, applied before paint as `html.pe-gradient`). Logo is the DEFAULT look (since Nov 19, later the same day; Gradient before it):  dev1.phytoelements.in's brand gradient (#0B7CF2 → #20B8D8 → #8FD14F, 110deg) on the top menu (original logo, dark links, dark Request button), buttons, bands and active states, with dark #07120A type and blue #0A66D6 labels; the footer stays light. Styles in `src/styles/gradient.css`. New components must look right in both.
- Logo look (`html.pe-logo`, `src/styles/logo.css`): ONLY the three logo colours — deep green #02584A (type; the one dark colour, used sparingly), leaf lime #94DE03 (buttons, active states), sun yellow #FDCE18 (highlights, testing marks) — on white and pale tints of them; product swatches become lime. No other hues in this look.
- Homepage hero review toggle (temporary, small pill on the right end of the hero's product ticker strip (bottom-right corner in Dev 1); takes no layout space): "Dev 2" (lot-testing hero, default on laptops/desktops >1120px) vs "Dev 1" (default on phones/tablets ≤1120px; photo-slit layout, photos and colours from dev1.phytoelements.in, same copy). `hero/HeroToggle`, `lib/hero-pref` (`html.hero-dev1`, stored on the device), styles `src/styles/hero-dev1.css`, photos in `public/images/dev1/` (from the client's earlier site).
- Homepage "From source to seal" (since Nov 19): ONE section, `sections/DockCarousel` — dev1's editorial photo carousel and photos ("From sourcing to your dock") carrying the five source-to-seal steps (Source, Screen, Test, Pass or reject, Label and supply) and the heading "Many lots come in. Only the ones that pass go out under our name." Copy in `company.ts` `dock` / `dockStages`, photos `public/images/dev1/`, styles `src/styles/dock.css`. Placed after "Who we are". The SealPipeline animation is off the homepage for now (still on /home-classic; SealStepper stays on Manufacturing & Quality). All looks.
- Gradient look section rhythm (every page): `main` sections alternate light paper #F6FAF1 / light green #EDF5E4 by position; only `.band` sections (important highlights) get the full gradient. Rules at the end of `gradient.css`. Light and Logo looks keep their own section colours.
- How we work (/operations): step cards `.ops-grid` with dev1's line icons (`ui/OpIcon`, `icon` field on `operatingFunctions`), number pill, bold display title above muted body.
- Logo artwork always shows in its original colours in every theme and look (the `-reverse` files are never displayed; rule at the end of verdan.css).
- Logo sun yellow #FDCE18 on inner pages (all looks): `PageHead` marks the title's last word (`.hl` yellow marker), section h2s after the page head get a short yellow underline, eyebrow stripes end in yellow, the current breadcrumb is underlined in yellow. Rules at the end of `logo.css`. Homepage untouched.
- Links to the page you are already on (logo / Home on "/", Products on /products …) glide back to the top (SmoothScroll); no reload, so the preloader does not replay.
- Hero ticker is linked to the lot animation (`hero/HeroTicker`, event `pe:specimen` from `SpecimenHero`): extracts in hero order (never numbered — no numbering on anything product-related, incl. carousel counts and rail navs); current one Lot in → Testing → Passed, earlier ones Passed for the round, later ones Awaiting; resets when the round restarts.
- Effects safety net (`lib/fx-guard`, `motion/FxGuard`, boot script in layout): ordinary scrolling (`html.pe-native`, remembered for the tab) when the browser lacks needed features (svh, clip-path circle, sticky, color-mix, IntersectionObserver/ResizeObserver → also `pe-calm`), the frame rate after load is under ~25 fps, three wheel gestures in a row don't move the page (PanelScroll watchdog), or a site script errors. PanelScroll never stays "gliding" past GLIDE+0.6s. SpecimenHero uses real elapsed time (dt cap 0.25s) so lots never crawl. package.json `browserslist` widened (Chrome 87+, Edge 88+, Firefox 78+, Safari 14+) for JS transpiling. Test pages in a fresh tab: sessionStorage `pe-native=1` forces the fallback.
